Before You Book

What You'll Need

Keep these ready and your pickup takes just a few minutes.

🪪

Driving Licence

A valid driving licence, at least one year old for self-drive.

🆔

ID Proof

Any government photo ID — Aadhaar, Passport or Voter ID.

💵

Advance Rent

An advance on the rental amount is collected at booking.

🔒

Security Deposit

A refundable security deposit of ₹5,000 per booking.

📏

250 km/day Limit

Each day includes 250 km; extra kilometres are chargeable.

⚠️ Please carry your original driving licence and ID proof at pickup — bookings cannot be handed over without them.
